## What this library is

Personality Engine is a C# library that maps host-tagged events to named affect channels and optional action-weight tints. Numeric gains, decay rates, and mix coefficients that are not taken from a cited paper are labeled **project convention**. They are game-feel knobs, not psychometric scoring keys.

Cited authors, publishers, and institutions (including without limitation McCrae & Costa, Mehrabian, Ortony, Clore & Collins, Gebhard / ALMA, Dias, Mascarenhas & Paiva / FAtiMA, Peterson, Skinner, Piaget, Erikson, and their publishers) have **not** endorsed this library, licensed their inventories or software to it, or reviewed its coefficients.
